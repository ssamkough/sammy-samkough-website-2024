# Ingredients

- Rice from your mom
- Vermicelli noodle
- Ghee (or butter as substitute)
- Water

<br />
# Method

<div style="margin-left: 1em;">
1. Get a cup of rice and rinse it with cold water until the water is clear.
2. Set the rice aside in a pool of water for 20 minutes.
3. 5 minutes before the rice is ready, heat up a pan with a tbsp of butter.
4. Whirl the butter around letting it melt, and dump in a half cup of vermicelli.
5. After it gets brown and toasted, toss in the rice. Whirl them around mixing it between one another.
6. Dump two cups of water and put a lid on it, let it simmer for 10 - 15 minutes.
</div>
