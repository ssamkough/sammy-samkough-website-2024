# Ingredients

- salmon
    - 2 tbsp olive oil
    - 3 tbsp soy sauce
    - 1 tbsp srirach
    - 2 tbsp honey
- cucumber salad
    - cucumber
    - avocado
    - red bell pepper
    - cilantro
    - lemon juice
    - sea salt

<br />
# Method

<div style="margin-left: 1em;">
1. Cut up salmon.
2. Mix in salmon base.
3. Bake at 425 degrees for 10 minutes.
4. Dice up stuffs for cucumber salad.
5. Mix in bowl.
6. Plate rice, salmon, then salad.
7. Drizzle with spicy mayo.
</div>
