# Ingredients

- [ ]  8 oz. Salmon

<br />
## Marinade

- [ ]  1 tbsp dark soy sauce
- [ ]  1 tbsp light soy sauce
- [ ]  1 tbsp rice wine vinegar
- [ ]  2 cloves garlic
- [ ]  1 inch piece ginger
- [ ]  1 tsp honey
- [ ]  1 tsp sriracha
- [ ]  1 tbsp olive oil

<br />
## Toppings

- [ ]  Scallions (optional)
- [ ]  Sesame seeds (optional)

<br />
## Additional

- [ ]  Rice
- [ ]  Cucumbers
- [ ]  Avocado
- [ ]  Thin carrot

<br />
# Method

<br />
## Prep

<div style="margin-left: 1em;">
1. Cut up veggies.
2. Cook some rice.
</div>

<br />
## Main

<div style="margin-left: 1em;">
1. Combine light soy sauce, dark soy sauce, ginger, garlic, olive oil / rice wine vinegar oil, honey to create marinade.
2. Pour marinade over salmon and let it sit for 15 minutes.
3. Toss salmon in oven at 375 degrees for 10 minutes.
4. Serve on a fresh bowl of rice with veggies, scallions, sesame seeds.
</div>
