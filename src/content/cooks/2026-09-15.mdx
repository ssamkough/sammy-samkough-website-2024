## Ingredients

- Shawarma style chicken
    - (6) chicken thighs, boneless
    - salt
    - kewpie mayo (2 spoonfuls)
    - cumin (a spoonful)
    - paprika (a spoonful)
    - garlic powder (a spoonful)
    - turmeric powder (a spoonful)
    - sumac (a spoonful)
    - black pepper (a spoonful)
    - ground cinnamon (a sprinkle)
    - cayenne pepper (a sprinkle)
- Yogurt sauce
    - yogurt, non-fat, plain
    - garlic, grated (2 cloves)
    - black pepper (a spoonful)
    - lemon juice (1/2 lemon)
    - fresh dill, minced (a spoonful)
    - salt
- Salad
    - romaine lettuce
    - cherry tomatoes, halved
    - cucumbers, cubed
    - feta
    - pickled onions
    - tortilla strips

<br/>
## Instructions

<div style="margin-left: 1em;">
1. Salt the chicken thighs with a even sprinkle of salt on all sides. In a small bowl, combine all of the spices with the mayo. Add the mayo marinade to the chicken and rub it all over.
2. To a ripping hot griddle or cast iron, add the chicken and sear until browned on both sides. Brown the chicken until it has reached around 170°F/ 76°C. Once the chicken is cooked, pull it off the heat and let it rest a bit before slicing.
3. Combine the yogurt, garlic, spices, and lemon juice. Mix it together and taste it. Adjust the salt and other seasonings to your preference.
4. Add the lettuce to a bowl and top with the tortilla strips or pita. Layer on the yogurt sauce, cucumber, tomato, feta, cheese, chicken, and pickled onion. Enjoy.
</div>
