# Ingredients

*Serves 2 to 4*

- [ ]  1 pound beef tenderloin, top sirloin, or skirt steak, thinly sliced (about ⅛ inch thick)
- [ ]  4 garlic cloves, minced
- [ ]  2 green onions, chopped
- [ ]  2 tablespoons soy sauce
- [ ]  2 tablespoons sugar
- [ ]  3 tablespoons water
- [ ]  1 tablespoon toasted sesame oil
- [ ]  1 tablespoon toasted sesame seeds, coarsely ground
- [ ]  ½ teaspoon ground black pepper

<br />
# Method

<br />
## Prep

<div style="margin-left: 1em;">
1. Slice beef (if not sliced already).
</div>

<br />
## Main

<div style="margin-left: 1em;">
1. Make marinade by combining the garlic, green onion, soy sauce, sugar, water, sesame oil, sesame seeds, and ground black pepper in a large bowl. Mix it well with a spoon until the sugar is well dissolved.
2. Add the sliced beef and mix everything by hand until the beef is covered in the marinade. Keep in the fridge for at least 30 minutes.
3. Heat a large skillet over high heat until very hot. Add the marinated beef and cook it quickly, stirring and turning until cooked through. Because the pan is already hot and the beef is thinly sliced, it cooks fast without releasing much liquid. You can also cook the beef on a grill. Transfer it to a plate or a cast iron plate to serve with rice, lettuce, ssamjang, and more side dishes.
4. To eat, wrap a spoonful of rice, a piece of bulgogi, and a little bit of ssamjang in a lettuce leaf. Fold it over and eat. Repeat!
</div>
