## Ingredients

- salmon
- salt
- pepper
- italian seasoning
- garlic
- vegetable oil
- lemon

<br/>
## Method

<div style="margin-left: 1em;">
1. Preheat oven to 350
2. Put 1/4th of salt and pepper in a bowl
3. Put 1 teaspoons of italian seasoning in same bowl
4. Put 2 teaspoons of oil in same bowl
5. Squeeze lemon into bowl
6. Crush 1-2 cloves of garlic and put it into bowl
7. Mix bowl until mixed well
8. Coat 1 salmon in this mixture well
9. Put salmon into oven for 12-15 minutes (let it get pink and a little crispy)
10. Broil it for 2-3 minutes for some more crispiness
</div>
